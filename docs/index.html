    About the data

    Finance & staffing: enrollment is final ADM; spending is Table A7 current expenditures by funding source, with per-pupil figures rounded half up; staffing is Table A6 total positions. The NCDPI year code is the school year's ending year and the state fiscal year: FY2025 = 2024–25.

    Student outcomes: 2025–26 End-of-Grade and End-of-Course results at the district (Public School Unit) level. Proficient is achievement level 3 or above (GLP); college & career ready is levels 4 and 5 (CCR). Figures cover all subjects, grades and assessment types for the selected student group. NCDPI suppresses percentages below 5 or above 95; suppressed cells are left blank.

    ROI is FY2025 total per-pupil spending divided by the share of students reaching grade-level proficiency in 2025–26 — the cost of one proficient student. Lower is better. The ranking is ascending. Shades of red show how far each district's cost sits above the median of its own OSP group (Large/Medium/Small) for the selected student group: pale at or below the median, darker at +10%, +25%, +50%, +100% and +150% or more.

    State + local + federal per-pupil differs from the total by up to $1 because each component is rounded independently. District boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line 2024; grey areas are North Carolina districts outside the 30-district study panel.
